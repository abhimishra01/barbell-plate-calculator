# Code review, maintainer lens: barbell-plate-calculator (Kim)

Verdict: Ship-ready core. The plate math is correct and tested (16 tests pass; I also checked it against a brute-force search for lb and kg sets, 0 mismatches). No must-fix items. The should-fix items are about wrong default numbers, two content overclaims, and a Tool.astro that nobody tests. Must-fix: 0.

Known template leftovers (README, new-tool.sh, tracked screenshots, duplicate legal links, build-date "Last updated", British "colour") are not repeated here. Also untracked and not in .gitignore: `.playwright-cli/` (add it before the next commit).

## Checks done

- Recomputed by hand and in code: 226 (closest 225, up 227.5), 227 (227.5), 226.25 (tie goes lighter), 185, 205, 275, 405 lb; 80, 140, 180, 200 kg; 102.5 kg (25 + 15 + 1.25); collar 2.5 with 20 kg bar at 100 kg; target at or under the bar; no plates; only 45 and 35 plates (300 lb gives 295 closest). All match the Article tables and the 225 lb worked example. Brute-force check for lb and a 3-plate set: 0 mismatches. The kg mismatches my script showed above 400 kg came from my own 10-plate limit, not from the code.
- Stress: 4999 lb with all plates 8 ms, with only a 1.25 plate 3 ms. No crash on 1e308 bar or collar.
- Safety: all `innerHTML` strings are built from fixed tables and numbers (`fmt`, `Number`). Stored values are never inserted as HTML. JSON-LD comes from static FAQ text. No injection path found.
- Template rules: canonical is correct on every page; pages.dev rules are in `_headers`; About, Contact, Privacy and Terms are linked in footer and on the home page; 404 has noindex and is out of the sitemap; GA and AdSense scripts are absent when the IDs are empty; `ads.txt` is a comment line.
- Not tested by me: browser behaviour (that is the regression and design reviewers' job).
