# Code review: readability (Hermione)

Verdict: a new engineer can follow this code, and the article tables and worked example match the engine (recomputed 17 loads and the 225 lb warm-up ramp, all correct, 16/16 tests pass). Two pieces of user-facing text are wrong or misleading, and several names mean two things at once. Must-fix: 1.

Known template leftovers (template README and `favicon.svg.ts` mention, `scripts/new-tool.sh`, tracked screenshots, duplicate legal links on the home page, build-date "Last updated", British spelling "personalised") are on the fixer's list and not repeated below.
